Privacy Policy

Kasatria 3D People Table is a demonstration web page built for a technical assignment.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, the page receives only your basic profile: name, email address and profile photo (the openid, email and profile scopes). No access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data is requested.

How it is used

Your name and photo are shown in the page header while you are signed in. That is the only use.

Storage and sharing

The page has no server and no database. Your sign-in token is kept only in your browser's session storage and is removed when you sign out or close the tab. Nothing is sent to, stored by, or shared with any third party, and no analytics or tracking is used.

Data shown on the page

The people shown in the 3D table come from a sample Google Sheet supplied with the assignment.
